## [DEA-01] Scope
21 CFR Part 1311 sets the rules for issuing prescriptions for controlled substances electronically (EPCS), in addition to the general rules for prescribing.

## [DEA-02] Prescriber identity proofing
A prescriber's identity must be proofed (verified) before the prescriber is given the ability to sign electronic prescriptions for controlled substances.

## [DEA-03] Two-factor authentication at signing
Each controlled-substance prescription must be signed using two-factor authentication at the moment of signing. One of the factors must come from a separate device or token.

## [DEA-04] Third-party audit of the software
The prescribing software must be audited by a third party before first use, again every 2 years, and whenever its functionality changes.

## [DEA-05] Notification when an audit fails
If an audit shows the software does not meet the requirements, the DEA must be notified within 1 business day.

## [DEA-06] Audit trail
The application must keep a full audit trail of the events relevant to creating, signing and transmitting controlled-substance prescriptions, so that compliance can be demonstrated.
